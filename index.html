Project SENTRI

An independent awareness initiative dedicated to making artificial intelligence more understandable, ethical, secure, and beneficial for everyone through accessible education and responsible dialogue.

Learn More Visit GitHub
Mission

Why SENTRI Exists

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the world faster than public understanding can keep pace.

Project SENTRI was created to bridge this gap by promoting awareness, encouraging critical thinking, and making AI concepts more accessible to everyone.

Through educational content, open resources, and community engagement, SENTRI aims to foster informed conversations about the future of artificial intelligence.

Core Pillars

Our Focus

Building awareness through education and responsible dialogue.

AI Safety

Understanding how AI systems can be designed, deployed, and governed safely.

AI Ethics

Exploring fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and human values in AI.

AI Security

Raising awareness about emerging risks, adversarial attacks, misuse, and secure AI development.

Public Awareness

Making AI understandable for students, educators, professionals, and the wider community.

Impact

Why This Matters

Artificial intelligence will influence nearly every aspect of society. Understanding AI is no longer optional—it is becoming an essential skill for informed citizens, students, policymakers, and future innovators.

Project SENTRI believes that responsible AI begins with informed people.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Project SENTRI and our educational initiative.

What is Project SENTRI?
Project SENTRI is an independent educational initiative focused on promoting public awareness around AI safety, ethics, and responsible development. We are not a company, startup, or SaaS platform.
Why focus on AI ethics?
As AI systems make more decisions that affect human lives, ensuring fairness, privacy, accountability, and alignment with human values becomes essential to prevent bias and harm.
Why focus on AI safety?
AI safety addresses how we build, deploy, and govern systems to ensure they remain dependable, secure, and beneficial without producing unintended consequences or security risks.
Can schools or organizations collaborate?
Yes. We welcome educators, academic institutions, and organizations to use our open learning resources, co-host discussions, or contribute educational content.
How can I support the initiative?
You can support Project SENTRI by sharing our educational resources, contributing through GitHub, suggesting improvements, or spreading awareness about responsible AI in your community.
